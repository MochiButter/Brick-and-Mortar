A simple block that cooks stone and clay twice as quickly as the furnace, with 3 hatches on every side but the face that allows you to make **4** different variants of any "cookable" bricks



### Features

* 4 variants of brick, the more hatches open the darker or "burnt" it is
* Placing soul sand under gives another shade of variants, usually blue-r or green-er depending on what it is
* Every brick has a tile variant, squares instead of rectangles
* Data-driven crafting recipes so you can add anything you want with a datapack, making it powerful for mudpack projects
